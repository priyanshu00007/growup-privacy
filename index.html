Privacy Policy

BlazeUp is built to help people lock in, level up, and never break the chain. This policy explains what we collect (basically nothing), why, and how we protect it.

Overview What we store How we use it Sharing Children Your rights Security Changes Contact
1 · Overview

Owner: Priyanshu (priyanshu_2004), operating as GrowUp App.

Service: BlazeUp — a mobile focus and productivity app available on iOS, Android, and web.

Scope: This policy covers the BlazeUp mobile app and any companion website or web version operated by GrowUp App. It does not cover third-party services we link to or that you choose to connect.

By using BlazeUp, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

The short version: BlazeUp does not collect, send, sell, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no tracking.

2 · What we store

BlazeUp stores data only locally on your device, inside the app. That data never leaves your phone or browser unless you explicitly choose to share something yourself through the system share sheet.

What stays on your device

  • Profile settings — your display name, exam name and date, daily goal, and weekly target. These are optional and only shown inside your own app.
  • Focus sessions — start time, end time, duration, whether the session was abandoned, and the tag or mood you picked. Used only to calculate your streak, stats, rank, and garden on your own device.
  • Mood check-ins — the mood you choose each day, stored with the date.
  • Streak and XP — derived only from your sessions, stored locally.
  • To-do tasks — tasks you add for yourself.
  • Materials — files or folders you choose to upload, kept on your device for your own use. We do not read, scan, or harvest the content of those files.

What we do not collect

  • No account. You do not need to sign up or log in.
  • No location. BlazeUp does not ask for location permission and does not use your location for any reason.
  • No contacts. BlazeUp does not ask for contacts permission and does not access your contacts.
  • No cloud sync. Your data is not uploaded anywhere.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, or device identifiers for tracking, advertising, or profiling.
  • No advertising. BlazeUp has no ads and does not build advertising profiles.
3 · How we use it

Because your data stays on your device, BlazeUp uses it only to give you the features you see:

  • Focus timer, streaks, ranks, XP, daily goals, and the study garden.
  • Your personalized home screen, including the exam countdown when you set one.
  • Your profile, to-do list, and any materials you upload for yourself.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not build profiles about you. We do not use it for anything that happens outside your device, except when you deliberately share something yourself.

4 · Sharing

BlazeUp does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone.

The only time anything leaves your device is when you choose to share something yourself — for example, sharing your forest or streak through the system share sheet. In that case, you control exactly what is shared and where it goes.

App stores (such as Apple and Google) are separate parties. Their own privacy practices cover the app download and any payment process, and are not covered by this policy.

5 · Children

BlazeUp is not intended for children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal information, there is nothing for us to delete if a child uses the app — the data simply lives on their own device and can be removed by deleting the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, use the contact details in the Contact section.

6 · Your rights

Since BlazeUp does not collect or store your data on our servers, the simplest way to exercise your rights is on your own device:

  • Delete your data — delete the app, or clear its local storage through your device settings. That removes your sessions, profile, to-dos, mood check-ins, and materials from your device.
  • No account to close — there is no account and no cloud profile tied to you.

If you ever believe BlazeUp is holding data it should not be, contact us using the details below and we will help where we can.

7 · Security

The biggest protection for your data is your device itself. Because BlazeUp keeps everything local, your data is only as accessible as your device is. We recommend:

  • Using a device passcode or lock.
  • Keeping the app and operating system updated.
  • Not handing your unlocked device to people you do not trust.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever learn that BlazeUp behaves in a way that puts user data at risk, we will act promptly to fix it.

8 · Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, new features, or legal requirements.

The Effective date at the top of this page shows the latest version. If we make a material change — especially one that affects how data is handled — we will try to notify you in the app or through update notes before the change takes effect.

Continued use of BlazeUp after a change means you accept the updated policy.
